Standards Repository

Every rule is anchored to international and national standards: SNA 2025, IMF GFS 2014, IMF BPM6, OECD BD4, ISIC Rev.4 and the wider stack.

SNA2025 — System of National Accounts 2025 (with SNA 2008 transition)
UN, IMF, OECD, World Bank, Eurostat · 2025 · National Accounts, Sectorisation, Business Demography

Institutional sector classification, residence, market/non-market production, ownership and control, enterprise groups.

ConceptDefinition
Institutional unitAn economic entity capable of owning assets, incurring liabilities, and engaging in economic activities in its own right.
ResidenceAn institutional unit is resident where it has its centre of predominant economic interest (≥1 year).
Market producerA producer whose sales cover more than 50% of production costs over a sustained period (economically significant prices).
GFS2014 — Government Finance Statistics Manual
International Monetary Fund · 2014 · Government Finance Statistics, Public Sector

Public-sector boundary; general government vs public corporations; controlled corporations; PPPs and SPVs.

ConceptDefinition
Public sector boundaryGeneral government plus public corporations controlled by government.
BPM6 — Balance of Payments and IIP Manual
International Monetary Fund · 6th · Balance of Payments, Residence

Residence, branches, balance of payments treatment, external sector boundary.

BD4 — OECD Benchmark Definition of Foreign Direct Investment
OECD · 4th · FDI, Ownership and Control

10% voting threshold, directional principle, ultimate investing country, ultimate controlling institutional unit.

ConceptDefinition
Direct investment relationshipEstablished at a 10% voting-power threshold; below is portfolio investment.
Ultimate Controlling Institutional Unit (UCI)The unit at the top of the ownership chain that is not itself controlled by another unit.
ISIC4 — International Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities
United Nations Statistics Division · Rev.4 · Economic Activity, Structural Business Statistics

Hierarchical economic-activity coding: section > division > group > class; principal/secondary/ancillary activity.

ConceptDefinition
Principal activityThe activity that generates the largest share of value added.
CPC — Central Product Classification
United Nations Statistics Division · 2.1 · Products, Trade

Product classification cross-walked to ISIC.

COFOG — Classification of the Functions of Government
UN/OECD · — · Government Finance Statistics

Functional classification of government expenditure.

ICSE — International Classification of Status in Employment
ILO · ICSE-18 · Labour, Employment

Status-in-employment classification.

SEEA — System of Environmental-Economic Accounting
UN · 2012 · Environment

Environmental-economic accounts; enterprise-level activity attribution.

LEI — Legal Entity Identifier (ISO 17442)
GLEIF / ISO · ISO 17442 · Identification, Business Register

Globally unique 20-character legal-entity identifier.

GSIM — Generic Statistical Information Model
UNECE · 1.2 · Metadata, Information Model

Reference framework for statistical information objects and metadata.

GSBPM — Generic Statistical Business Process Model
UNECE · 5.1 · Process

Standard model of the statistical production process.

SDMX — Statistical Data and Metadata eXchange
SDMX Initiative · 3.0 · Exchange, Dissemination

Standards for exchanging statistical data and metadata.

IRIS — International Recommendations for Industrial Statistics
UNSD · 2008 · Industrial Statistics

Recommendations for industrial statistics.

IRBR — International / UNSD-Eurostat Recommendations for Business Registers
UNSD/Eurostat · — · Business Register

Statistical business register units, coverage, and maintenance.
